## Executive Summary

This report establishes the delivery structure, live instruction hours, effective hourly rates across all 6 Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) tiers, and revenue economics for all three core training offerings provided by The Digital Futurist:

1. **Flagship Business Training**: 60 days total (30 days live engineering training + 30 days dedicated support), up to 10 participants included (+$300/extra seat up to 20 seats max).
2. **Flagship Personal Training**: 60 days total (30 days live 1-on-1 training + 30 days dedicated support), strictly 1-on-1 private acceleration.
3. **Individual Service Picks**: 10 days total (5 days live training + 5 days dedicated support), available for individuals (strictly 1 person, $300 base) and businesses (1 seat included, +$300/extra seat up to 10 seats max, $300 base).

All live training runs on **business days** (Monday to Friday, excluding Saturdays and Sundays) at **4 hours per day** (30 working days over 6-8 weeks).

## 4. Strategic Observations & Capacity Notes

1. **Accessibility and Mission Alignment:**
   - At $25.00/hr for Business Flagship ($2.50/hr per seat for 10 participants) and $25.00/hr for 1-on-1 Personal Flagship, these rates provide senior Generative AI training at global parity.
   - For developing nations (Tier 2 and Tier 3), rates ensure no high-impact engineering team or individual is priced out of foundational AI architectural skills.

2. **Delivery Bandwidth & Solo Capacity:**
   - With 120 live training hours per flagship program (4 hours/day over 30 business days, or 6-8 calendar weeks), running one Business Flagship and one Personal Flagship simultaneously requires **8 live teaching hours per day**.
   - Solo practice capacity is capped at 1-2 concurrent flagship clients per month to maintain training quality, bespoke repository code reviews, and student responsiveness.

3. **Installment & Cashflow Architecture:**
   - **Business Flagship ($3,000 USD):** Five equal payments of $600 USD. Walk away owing nothing after the first 5 days. Dedicated 30-day support period is $0 USD (100% included in the package).
   - **Personal Flagship ($3,000 USD):** Five equal payments of $600 USD. Walk away owing nothing after the first 5 days. Dedicated 30-day support period is $0 USD (100% included in the package).
   - **Individual Services ($300 USD):** Billed across 5 milestones (Days 1 to 5, $60 USD per milestone); zero upfront advance required. Walk away owing nothing after Day 1. Dedicated 5-day support period is $0 USD (100% included in the service rate).

4. **Open-Source Licensing (Apache 2.0):**
