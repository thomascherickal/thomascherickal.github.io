Common Questions

Collaboration FAQs

Transparent answers about process, scheduling, intellectual property, and communication.

How quickly can an engagement start?

Single engagements and workshops can typically be scheduled within 5 to 10 business days of scope approval. Monthly retainers and multi-part educational curricula are scheduled based on current sprint capacity.

What communication channels do you support?

Communication is conducted asynchronously via shared Slack or Discord channels, email, and GitHub Pull Requests. For live training, interactive sessions are hosted on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams.

How does runtime code verification work?

Every code sample included in tutorials, documentation, and training modules is tested in live execution environments (Python REPLs, cargo test sandboxes, go test suites, or Mojo test runners) before delivery.

Who owns the intellectual property (IP)?

Upon milestone payment completion, full commercial publication rights to the commissioned technical deliverables transfer to your organization, with author byline attribution preserved under our editorial policy.

Do you accept ghostwriting commissions?

No. I operate under a strict editorial integrity policy: all articles, guides, and technical benchmarks carry my authentic byline. This preserves developer trust and maximizes authentic social and domain authority.

What are the 6 Tiers of Payment?

Pricing is organized across 6 tiers of payment: Tier 1 Developed Nations (100%), Tier 2 Developing Nations (75%), Tier 3 Least Developed Nations (50%), Tier 4 Startups (50% off), Tier 5 Non-Profits & Academia (subsidized custom rates), and Tier 6 Third-World Countries & Underprivileged Individuals (75% off).
