## Executive Summary

This report establishes the delivery structure, live instruction hours, effective hourly rates across all 6 Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) tiers, and revenue economics for all three core training offerings provided by The Digital Futurist:

1. **Flagship Business Training**: 70 days total (30 days live training + 30 days guidance, Q&A and written guides), up to 25 participants included (+$200/extra seat).
2. **Flagship Personal Training**: 60 days total (30 days live training + 30 days guidance, Q&A and written guides), strictly 1-on-1 private acceleration.
3. **Individual Service Picks**: 10 days total (5 days live training + 5 days dedicated support), available for individuals (1-on-1) and businesses (up to 25 participants, +$200/extra seat).

All live training runs on **business days** (Monday to Friday, excluding Saturdays and Sundays) at **4 hours per day** (30 working days over 6-8 weeks with 8 - 10 days as holidays and recovery days).

## 4. Strategic Observations & Capacity Notes

1. **Accessibility and Mission Alignment:**
   - At $16.67/hr for Business Flagship ($0.67/hr per seat for 25 participants) and $8.33/hr for 1-on-1 Personal Flagship, these rates provide senior Generative AI training at global parity.
   - For developing nations (Tier 2 and Tier 3), rates ensure no high-impact engineering team or individual is priced out of foundational AI architectural skills.

2. **Delivery Bandwidth & Solo Capacity:**
   - With 120 live training hours per flagship program (4 hours/day over 30 business days, or 6-8 calendar weeks), running one Business Flagship and one Personal Flagship simultaneously requires **8 live teaching hours per day**.
   - Solo practice capacity is capped at 1 concurrent flagship clients per month to maintain training quality, bespoke repository code reviews, and student responsiveness.

3. **Installment & Cashflow Architecture:**
   - **Business Flagship ($2,000 USD):** Five equal payments of $400 USD. Walk away owing nothing after the first 5 days.
   - **Personal Flagship ($1,000 USD):** Five equal payments of $200 USD. Walk away owing nothing after the first 5 days.
   - **Individual Services ($100 / $200 USD):** Billed across 5 milestones (Days 1 to 5); zero upfront advance required. Walk away owing nothing after Day 1.

4. **Open-Source Licensing (Apache 2.0):**
   - All written guides, training materials, operating manuals, tutorials, curriculum documentation, and deep dives authored by Thomas Cherickal fall under the permissive **Apache 2.0 license**, permitting unrestricted commercial use, adaptation, and internal deployment with attribution.
