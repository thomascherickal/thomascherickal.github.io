## 1. Service
 Overview & Scope

- Daily Work Automation: Finding the repetitive tasks in sales, operations, finance and
 engineering, and automating the ones Claude can do reliably.
- Claude Skills and Plugins: Turning each team's repeated procedures into skills, and
 bundling skills, connectors and commands into plugins the whole team can install.
- Microsoft 365 and Connectors: Using Claude in Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Outlook, plus
 connectors to Google Workspace, Slack, Jira and other tools your teams already use.
- Self-Hosted Option: Python automation scripts and local models (Ollama, LM Studio) on
 Linux and Docker for work that must stay on your own machines.
- Staff Enablement: Live training so non-technical staff and developers can build, test and
 maintain their own automations.

## 2. Standard
 Architecture & Tools

- Automation Platform: Claude with skills, plugins and scheduled tasks, plus Claude in
 Chrome for web tools that have no connector.
- AI Models: Claude through the apps and the Anthropic Python SDK, and local models through
 Ollama or LM Studio.
- Connectors: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Jira and custom MCP servers in Python
 for internal systems.
- Data Security: Per-user connector permissions, write actions set to Needs approval, and
 local processing where data must stay in-house.
