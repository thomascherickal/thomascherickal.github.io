# Country-Wise Parity Pricing (195 Countries)

All 195 sovereign countries (193 UN member states plus Holy See and State of Palestine) are mapped to our Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) tiers based on the UN 2026 member roster and World Bank FY27 (effective July 1, 2026 - June 30, 2027) GNI per capita classifications.

For full program details, payment milestones ($0 upfront, 20% in five installments), and the walkaway guarantee, see [Pricing Overview](pricing.html).

## Pricing Summary Across All 6 Tiers

- **Tier 1 (100% - Developed)**: Flagship `$3,000 USD` | Individual Service `$300 USD` | Extra Seat `+$300 USD`
- **Tier 2 (75% - Upper-Middle)**: Flagship `$2,250 USD` | Individual Service `$225 USD` | Extra Seat `+$225 USD`
- **Tier 3 (50% - Lower-Middle)**: Flagship `$1,500 USD` | Individual Service `$150 USD` | Extra Seat `+$150 USD`
- **Tier 4 (50% - Startups & Founders)**: Flagship `$1,500 USD` | Individual Service `$150 USD` | Extra Seat `+$150 USD` (open to early-stage companies in any country)
- **Tier 5 (25% - Academia & Non-Profits)**: Flagship `$750 USD` | Individual Service `$75 USD` | Extra Seat `+$75 USD` (open to colleges, universities, and registered non-profits in any country)
- **Tier 6 (Custom - Relief & Low-Income)**: Custom subsidized pricing for low-income economies and crisis-affected regions
