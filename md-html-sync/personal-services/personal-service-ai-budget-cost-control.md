## Tiered Model Routing Architecture

Not every prompt deserves a frontier model. By introducing a lightweight router in front of your scripts, you can classify each request by complexity before it ever leaves your machine. Routine tasks - such as extracting dates from an email or generating commit messages - are handled locally by Ollama. Medium-complexity tasks route to fast cloud models like Gemini Flash or Claude Haiku. Only high-stakes architectural reasoning or complex debugging escalates to frontier models like Claude Opus or Gemini Pro.
