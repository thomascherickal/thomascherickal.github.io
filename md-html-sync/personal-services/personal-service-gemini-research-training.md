## Personal Research Productivity Architecture and Zettelkasten Sync

Deep technical research fails when insights remain trapped inside ephemeral chat threads. A personal Gemini research architecture treats every synthesis session as a structured ingestion pipeline into your permanent knowledge base, such as an Obsidian vault or a local Markdown Zettelkasten. Instead of manually copying paragraphs, your pipeline outputs atomic Markdown notes with YAML frontmatter containing source DOIs, publication dates, confidence scores, and bidirectional wiki-links.

By pairing Gemini's long-context window with Gemini Context Caching, you can pin a 500,000-token corpus of PDFs, RFCs, and benchmark tables in memory for hours while paying a fraction of standard input token rates. You then run batched extraction passes overnight: one pass extracts methodology claims, a second pass extracts quantitative baselines, and a third pass generates cross-reference links against your existing local notes.
