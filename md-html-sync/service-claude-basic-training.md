## 1. Service
 Overview & Scope

- Claude Models & Selection: When to use Haiku (fast, low-cost tasks), Sonnet (most
 everyday work), and Opus or Fable (harder reasoning and long research).
- Effort Levels: Using the effort setting, from low to max, to trade reasoning depth
 against speed and cost.
- Structured Prompting & XML Tags: Clear roles, worked examples, and XML tags such as
 <context>, <instructions> and <example> that separate the parts of a prompt.
- Projects, Artifacts & Skills: Team Projects holding brand guidelines and SOPs, live
 documents and charts as Artifacts, and skills for repeated tasks.
- Data Privacy & Boundaries: What your plan's data settings mean, what never to paste
 into AI tools, and how connector permissions limit access.
