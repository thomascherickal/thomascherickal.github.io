## Automated Scientific Literature Synthesis

Scientific knowledge expands faster than human researchers can read. Claude connects programmatically to arXiv, PubMed, and Semantic Scholar APIs to ingest thousands of peer-reviewed publications, extracting experimental methodologies, reconciling contradictory findings, and generating comprehensive state-of-the-art literature reviews in minutes. Automated knowledge graphs visualize latent relationships between targets and trial outcomes.

- Cross-Disciplinary Synthesis: Connect insights across molecular biology, material physics, and computer science to surface hidden structural parallels.
- Methodology Extraction: Isolate exact laboratory protocols, reagent concentrations, and statistical benchmarks from dense PDF appendices.
- Citation Graph Auditing: Track citation provenance to flag retracted studies, methodological flaws, and unverified empirical assertions.

## Closed-Loop Hypothesis Generation & In-Silico Modeling

Claude advances computational discovery through closed-loop hypothesis formulation. By analyzing empirical observations, Claude formulates falsifiable mathematical hypotheses, translates them into vectorized Python simulations using NumPy and SciPy, and executes in-silico experiments inside sandboxed runtimes, systematically refining parameters based on simulation residuals. During computational screening, Claude calculates Gibbs free energy and binding affinities to prioritize viable synthesis candidates.

---

## Reproducibility Audits & Automated Verification

To combat the scientific reproducibility crisis, Claude performs rigorous statistical audits on experimental datasets. The agent re-runs published analysis scripts in isolated environments, validates p-values and confidence intervals, and flags statistical p-hacking, producing reproducible Jupyter notebooks and interactive Artifacts ready for peer-reviewed publication. Every generated chart, regression slope, and mathematical derivation is paired with verifiable provenance tracking back to raw experimental sensors, meeting rigorous peer-review standards.
