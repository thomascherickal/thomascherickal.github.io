## 1. Simple Pricing, Clear Schedule, and Zero Upfront Cost

Hiring consultants can feel risky when they ask for large deposits upfront. We make it completely safe and
 simple for you. You never pay before seeing real results.

If you are happy with the results and do not want additional training, you can walk away after any phase of the
 training, pay only for the phases that have been completed, and keep the shared materials. However, the support
 period and the Discord community will not be provided if this happens.

Here is exactly how our pricing and schedule work in simple points:

1. Strictly Zero Dollars Upfront ($0): You never pay a single cent before training begins.
 There are no registration fees, no setup fees, and no advance deposits.
2. Pay Training in Ten Equal Parts (10% Each): Your live training investment is divided into
 ten equal payments of 10% each. You only pay after each training milestone is completed and verified by you.
3. Zero Charge Training Materials: Zero charge for training materials: All training materials
 will be provided to the client with an Apache 2.0 license for internal use, commercial use, or even resale
 which requires attribution and keeping the licence and notice files. The Apache 2.0 licence is granted only on
 full completion, and never if the client walks away at any stage. The license will not be provided if the
 training ends early.
4. Walk Away Free Guarantee: Try the training with zero financial risk. If you are not
 completely happy after the first phase, you can walk away owing nothing.
5. Clear Daily Schedule: Training takes place for 4 hours each day, Monday through Friday.
 Saturdays and Sundays are restful holidays. Every session is delivered 100% online so anyone worldwide can
 join.
6. Full Commercial Ownership (Apache 2.0): Everything created during the training - all
 written notes, guides, checklists, and code - belongs to you under the open Apache 2.0 license. You can use it
 inside your business, sell it to clients, or share it however you want.
