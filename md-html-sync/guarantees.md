## 1. Simple Pricing, Clear Schedule, and Zero Upfront Cost

Hiring consultants can feel risky when they ask for large deposits upfront. We make it completely safe and simple for you. You never pay before seeing real results. Here is exactly how our pricing and schedule work in simple points:

2. Pay Training in Five Equal Parts (20% Each): Your live training investment is divided into five equal payments of 20% each. You only pay after each training milestone is completed and verified by you.
3. Verified Support Period Payments: Dedicated support also requires strictly zero upfront money. For individual services, 5 days of support is an additional $50 USD due after 5 days of support.
4. Walk Away Free Guarantee: Try the training with zero financial risk. If you are not completely happy after the first phase, you can walk away owing nothing.
5. Clear Daily Schedule: Training takes place for 4 hours each day, Monday through Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are restful holidays. Every session is delivered 100% online so anyone worldwide can join.
6. Full Commercial Ownership (Apache 2.0): Everything created during the training - all written notes, guides, checklists, and code - belongs to you under the open Apache 2.0 license. You can use it inside your business, sell it to clients, or share it however you want.
