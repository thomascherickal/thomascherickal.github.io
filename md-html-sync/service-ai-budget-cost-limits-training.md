# 💲 AI Budget & Cost Limits Training (Token Governance & Free Models)
## Enterprise AI Transformation Service — Thomas Cherickal

> **// SERVICE SPECIFICATION 09**  
> **Training employees across all organizational levels on financial AI governance: establishing hard spending caps, API token budgeting, prompt compression, and routing minor tasks to free models.**

## 1. Service
 Overview & Scope

- Cross-Organizational Financial AI Literacy:

Educating employees, project managers, developers, and department heads on how cloud AI billing actually works (input vs. output tokens, context caching, reasoning tokens).
Demystifying token economics to prevent accidental runaway bills from oversized context windows, recursive agent loops, and unoptimized prompts.
Creating organizational awareness of cost tiers across proprietary and open models.
