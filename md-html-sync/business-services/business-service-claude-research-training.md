## Enterprise Claude Research Architecture

Most organizations adopt generative AI through ad-hoc browser chat sessions. Analysts paste sensitive spreadsheets into personal tabs, product managers craft one-off prompts that nobody else can reproduce, and engineering teams have zero visibility into token spend, hallucination rates, or data leakage. When an enterprise scales research across legal, R&D, security, and corporate strategy, individual chat habits become an operational liability.

Enterprise Claude Research & Training replaces fragmented prompting with a centralized, auditable research plane. Built on the Anthropic Messages API and governed by strict identity, data-retention, and citation controls, this architecture treats research workflows as version-controlled software systems. Every query passes through an enterprise gateway that enforces Single Sign-On (SSO), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), automated PII redaction, and OpenTelemetry trace logging before reaching Claude.

Instead of relying on opaque web interfaces, teams interact with curated research pipelines through Slack, internal portals, Claude Desktop, or IDE integrations. Underlying those interfaces is a deterministic orchestration layer that routes queries to the right model tier - pairing Claude Haiku for high-throughput triage, Claude Sonnet for structured extraction and synthesis, and Claude Opus for deep architectural or legal reasoning.

Enterprise research workloads exhibit wildly different complexity profiles. Classifying incoming support tickets or filtering ten thousand patent abstracts does not require frontier-level reasoning, whereas reconciling conflicting indemnity clauses across a merger data room demands maximum analytical depth. Our gateway implements deterministic semantic routing: lightweight classifiers running on Claude Haiku score query complexity and data sensitivity, dispatching routine extraction tasks to fast tiers while escalating multi-hop synthesis to Claude Sonnet or Claude Opus.

Equally critical is organizational workspace isolation. Mergers and acquisitions teams, human resources, and core R&D cannot share vector indices, prompt caches, or API logs. We partition Anthropic API keys, MCP server endpoints, and OpenTelemetry trace sinks by department boundary, ensuring strict tenant isolation across every research artifact.
