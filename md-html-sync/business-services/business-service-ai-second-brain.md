## Plain-Text Markdown & Git Vault Architecture

Proprietary SaaS knowledge bases trap your company's intellectual property behind proprietary databases and unpredictable subscription tiers. By standardizing on Obsidian Markdown vaults backed by Git, your team gains full cryptographic history, branch-based collaboration, and instant portability.

We structure the vault using a disciplined PARA hierarchy (`00-inbox`, `10-projects`, `20-areas`, `30-resources`, `90-archive`) paired with strict YAML frontmatter schemas (`title`, `date`, `tags`, `source`, `status`). A curated taxonomy of at most twenty canonical tags prevents tag sprawl and ensures deterministic filtering.
