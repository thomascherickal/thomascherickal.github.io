## Workflow Audits and Build-Versus-Buy Architecture Decisions

Every transformation begins with a two-week workflow audit across operations, sales, support, finance, and engineering. We score candidate workflows on four concrete criteria: weekly hours consumed, data readiness, error tolerance, and compliance sensitivity. High-frequency, text-heavy workflows with clean internal documentation go to the top of the queue; high-liability decisions with messy data stay human-led until foundations are ready.

Next comes the build-versus-buy decision. Companies waste huge budgets either building custom software for commodity needs (like meeting transcription) or buying rigid SaaS wrappers for their core competitive differentiators. We establish a clear decision matrix: buy off-the-shelf for generic productivity, and build lightweight, vendor-neutral pipelines (owning your prompts, hybrid RAG index, and evaluation suites) for workflows tied directly to your proprietary data and customer experience.
