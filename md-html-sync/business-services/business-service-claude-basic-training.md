- Interactive Artifacts: Building, viewing, and iterating on live standalone artifacts including interactive React dashboards, SVG schematics, and structured reports directly beside the chat.
- Claude Cowork in Microsoft 365: Integrating Claude with Excel spreadsheets for financial modeling and formula auditing, Word for drafting executive summaries, PowerPoint for slide outlines, and Outlook for inbox triage.
- Structured Prompt Architecture & XML Tags: Mastering role prompts, task framing, few-shot demonstration blocks, and semantic XML tags (<context>, <instructions>, <output_format>) for deterministic outputs.
- Enterprise Data Governance & Security: Enforcing strict zero-data-retention standards, verifying privacy controls, and configuring workspace-level seat management.
