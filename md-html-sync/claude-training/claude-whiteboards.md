## Table of Contents

Technical meetings and strategic brainstorms often generate immense cognitive value that evaporates minutes after the call ends. Disorganized notes, divergent stakeholder interpretations, and ambiguous action items stall momentum. Claude Whiteboards revolutionizes this transition: taking unstructured conversation transcripts or whiteboard snapshots and compiling them into clear, version-controlled visual diagrams explicitly marked with project phases, milestones, and technical deliverables that directly drive downstream automation.
