## Table of Contents

One of the most persistent frictions in enterprise generative AI is context amnesia. In traditional chatbot environments, every new conversation starts with a blank slate. Developers and knowledge workers waste countless hours re-pasting background documents, re-explaining architectural constraints, and re-uploading the same core specifications. Claude Projects solves this fundamental challenge by creating dedicated, persistent workspaces with up to 200,000 tokens of curated project files and customized instructions that persist indefinitely across sessions.
