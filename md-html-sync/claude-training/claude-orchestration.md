## Table of Contents

A single agent trying to research ten files, write three modules, update documentation, and verify security rules all in one giant conversation quickly loses focus. Multi-agent orchestration solves this by splitting complex work into small, independent roles - a coordinator plans and tracks progress while focused worker agents execute in parallel and pass results through strict verification gates.
